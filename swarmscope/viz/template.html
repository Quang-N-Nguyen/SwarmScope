Follow an item

Pick an item (a credential or dead-drop on redacted logs; a file, commit, coined term or role claim on named logs). Arrows show who probably passed it to whom, and appear when each agent adopts it. Press play.

Reveal

Cumulative shows everything up to the cursor. Sliding window shows only recent activity, so you can watch bursts pass.

Edges

Minimum tie strength

Tie strength is the Adamic–Adar score: a resource everyone uses adds little, a resource only two agents share adds a lot. Raise it to keep only the telling ties.

Highlight mechanism

Agents that used the chosen mechanism get a ring; the rest dim.

Selected node

Click a node to inspect it. Stop the replay at any moment and click an agent to see what it had done by then.

What happened, hour by hour

All charts share one clock (UTC). Grey lines are the milestones (operator announcements or new goals), when the log has them. Hover a chart to read the values.
